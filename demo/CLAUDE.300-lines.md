## How I like to work

I like things short and direct. I do not like long emails. I do not like long documents. If you can say it in three sentences say it in three sentences.

I want options with a recommendation. Do not give me a menu of five things with no opinion. Tell me what you would do.

Numbers before adjectives. "Volume is up 12%" not "volume is strong."

I think out loud a lot. If I ramble, pull out the decision.

I like to see the downside first. Tell me what can go wrong before you tell me the upside.

I am a morning person. My best thinking is before 10. After 3 PM I am mostly reacting.

I read on my phone a lot so format for a phone screen. Short paragraphs. Bullets are fine but not nested bullets.

Be flexible about customer calls. Customers come first.

I do not want you to be overly formal. I also do not want you to be casual. Somewhere in between.

When I ask you a question, answer the question first and then explain. Do not explain and then answer.

## Writing style

I write short. Two or three sentences for anything routine. I start with "Hi" and the first name. I sign off "Thanks, Jamie". For the board I am more formal but still short.

Words I never use: "circle back", "reach out", "touch base", "synergy", "leverage" as a verb, "going forward", "at the end of the day".

I use contractions. I do not use exclamation points. I do not use emoji.

When I disagree with someone I say so directly but I say what I agree with first.

For bad news I lead with the news, then the cause, then what we are doing.

I do not write "I hope this finds you well." Ever.
